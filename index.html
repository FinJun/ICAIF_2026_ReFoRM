01 / THE IDEA

Remember the reasoning.
Reconsider the relationships.

A company reports earnings every quarter. Inflation is released every month. Why should an AI forecaster start from scratch every time?

Past events offer more than an answer. They reveal which factors mattered, how those factors connected, and which conditions made a prediction plausible.

ReFoRM turns those connections into reusable reasoning paths. For a new event, it checks each relationship against current evidence—keeping, reversing, rejecting, or leaving it unresolved.

      What makes the reasoning better?

      The paper evaluates evidence grounding, logical validity, prediction alignment, probability justification, and mechanism specificity. ReFoRM scores highest on all five in this analysis.

      How broad is this evidence?

      The reasoning-quality evaluation covers one Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite run, with LLM judges rather than human ground truth. The paper treats it as supporting evidence, not a benchmark-wide estimate.

      Does every financial category improve?

      ReFoRM beats Direct Forecast on Brier score in four of five categories. Energy and commodities are nearly tied: 0.2186 for ReFoRM versus 0.2183 for Direct Forecast. These category results use one run per model (Table 4).

      What should we take away?

      Historical reasoning is useful when its relationships are rechecked. The study supports that approach on recurring financial events; broader domains and expert-curated reasoning structures remain directions for future research.
